# Marketing Optimization Playbook — Social Media Analytics Platform

## Platform Strategy Guide

### Instagram
**Best for:** D2C brands, Fashion, Youth audience (18-34), Visual products
**Recommended campaign types:** Reels, Stories, Carousel
**Optimal campaign duration:** 14-30 days
**Budget allocation tip:** Allocate 30-40% of total social budget if targeting 18-34 demographic
**Influencer strategy:** Influencer campaigns on Instagram typically outperform 
non-influencer campaigns by 15-25% in engagement rate
**CTR benchmark:** 0.8-1.5% is healthy. Above 2% is excellent.
**Common mistake:** Running static image ads when Reels outperform by 2-3x on this platform

### Facebook
**Best for:** Broad audiences, Retargeting, All age groups (25-55)
**Recommended campaign types:** Carousel, Video, Lead Generation
**Optimal campaign duration:** 21-45 days
**Budget allocation tip:** Best platform for retargeting warm audiences
**Audience tip:** Lookalike audiences based on top converters typically reduce CAC by 20-30%
**CTR benchmark:** 0.9-2% is healthy
**Common mistake:** Ignoring the 35-54 age segment which often has highest purchase intent

### YouTube
**Best for:** Brand awareness, Product demonstrations, Long-form content
**Recommended campaign types:** TrueView, Bumper Ads, Discovery Ads
**Optimal campaign duration:** 30-60 days
**Budget allocation tip:** Lower CPC than Instagram/LinkedIn but longer conversion cycle
**ROAS expectation:** Lower immediate ROAS but higher brand recall
**CTR benchmark:** 0.3-0.7% — lower CTR is normal for video discovery
**Common mistake:** Measuring YouTube by direct conversion rate instead of view-through conversions

### LinkedIn
**Best for:** B2B companies, SaaS, Professional services, High-income audiences
**Recommended campaign types:** Sponsored Content, Lead Gen Forms, Message Ads
**Optimal campaign duration:** 30-60 days
**Budget note:** Highest CPC platform (₹60-150) but highest quality B2B leads
**ROAS expectation:** Lower volume but higher order values
**Audience tip:** Job title and company size targeting reduces wasted spend significantly
**CTR benchmark:** 0.3-0.6% is healthy for LinkedIn
**Common mistake:** Using B2C creative style on a B2B platform

### WhatsApp
**Best for:** Retargeting, Flash sales, High-intent warm audiences
**Recommended campaign types:** Click-to-WhatsApp, Retargeting campaigns
**Optimal campaign duration:** 7-14 days (short burst campaigns work best)
**Budget allocation tip:** Lowest CPC platform — ideal for retargeting budget
**CTR benchmark:** 2-5% — highest CTR of all platforms due to warm audience intent
**Common mistake:** Using WhatsApp for cold audience acquisition instead of retargeting

## Campaign Optimization Rules

### When ROAS drops below 3.0
1. Audit audience targeting — check frequency caps (>3 = audience fatigue)
2. Rotate creative — static ads lose effectiveness after 2-3 weeks
3. Review landing page — slow load time kills conversion rate
4. Check competitor activity — increased competition raises CPC
5. Consider pausing lowest performing 20% of ad sets

### When CTR drops below 1%
1. Creative is fatigued — audience has seen it too many times
2. Launch A/B test with 3 new creative concepts immediately
3. Test video vs static — video typically lifts CTR by 30-50%
4. Narrow audience targeting — broad audiences have lower relevance scores
5. Review ad copy — headline is the most impactful element for CTR

### When Conversion Rate drops below 2%
1. Landing page audit — check for mobile responsiveness issues
2. Offer alignment — ensure ad promise matches landing page offer
3. Page speed — every 1 second delay reduces conversions by 7%
4. Trust signals — add reviews, testimonials, security badges
5. Exit intent — implement exit popup with special offer

### When CAC is rising
1. Shift budget to highest ROAS campaigns
2. Increase retargeting budget — existing warm audiences convert cheaper
3. Optimize for value-based bidding instead of volume
4. Review customer LTV — rising CAC is acceptable if LTV also rises
5. Test new lookalike audiences based on top 1% customers

## Seasonal Campaign Strategy

### Diwali Season (Oct-Nov)
- Highest revenue period — increase budget 30-50%
- Start campaigns 3-4 weeks before the festival
- Focus on gift categories, premium products
- Instagram and Facebook perform best
- Use countdown timers and urgency messaging

### Summer Season (Apr-Jun)
- Focus on experience and lifestyle products
- YouTube performs well for summer campaign brand awareness
- Younger audiences (18-24) are most active
- Test influencer collaborations for authentic summer content

### Regular Season
- Maintain baseline spend — do not cut budget entirely
- Focus on retargeting and loyalty campaigns
- Use this period for creative testing and audience building
- Build remarketing lists for upcoming peak seasons

## Audience Optimization Guide

### Age Group Strategy
- **18-24:** High CTR, lower conversion rate, mobile-first, Instagram/YouTube
- **25-34:** Best balance of CTR and conversion rate, all platforms
- **35-44:** Highest conversion rate and order value, Facebook/LinkedIn
- **45+:** Lower CTR but highest loyalty and repeat purchase rate

### Device Optimization
- Mobile campaigns: Shorter copy, vertical video, tap-friendly CTAs
- Desktop campaigns: Longer copy, detailed product showcases, comparison tables
- Tablet users: Hybrid approach, often highest conversion rate per session

### Gender Targeting
- Test gender-neutral campaigns first before splitting budgets
- Only split by gender if data shows >15% performance difference
- Avoid stereotyping — let data guide gender targeting decisions
